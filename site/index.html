02 / THE FOUNDATION

Start faster.
Keep your freedom.

A neutral UI layer, with clear boundaries
between your interface and your application.

01

Brand first. Not brand locked.

Semantic tokens for color, typography, spacing, density, and shape. No required visible kit badge is intended; legal notices still apply.

02

React at the core.

Owned primitives and CSS Modules. Reusable UI stays separate from routing, data fetching, forms, and framework-specific wiring.

03

Direction is foundational.

Logical CSS, bidi-aware text, and native Intl formatting. Arabic, English, and French are the initial localization targets.
